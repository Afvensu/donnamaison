# Donnamaison

Build the frontend for the Donna Maison luxury online store based on the attached prototype (donna-maison-prototype.html), applying the Brain Glue copy and persuasion enhancements:

1. Hero:
- Headline: "Quietly powerful. Undeniably her."
- Subtext: "Tailoring that commands the room before you speak. Hand-finished in Lagos. Cut for real curves from size 6 to 20."
- CTAs: "Step into the Collection" and "Find Your Fit (Sizes 6–20)"

2. Product details & pricing:
- Anchor the investment value and craftsmanship on garments (e.g., "Hand-placed Akwete silk crepe · Built to outlast five seasons of fast fashion · Complimentary atelier fitting included").
- Transparent fit notes, sizing brackets (UK 6–20), fabric composition, and occasion tags.

3. The Atelier Ledger (Newsletter / VIP access):
- Headline: "The Atelier Ledger"
- Copy: "We cut in runs of twenty, not twenty thousand. Members receive private allocations 48 hours before public release, direct styling advice from our patternmakers, and invitations to seasonal fittings."
- CTA: "Request an Invitation"

4. Interactive frontend storefront:
- Occasion navigation ("Hold the room", "Be the photograph", "Softest kind of armour")
- Product grid with filterable categories, quick view, and rich product detail drawer/page
- Interactive shopping bag drawer with size selector, fit guidance, and order summary
- Atelier story, craftsmanship highlights, and fit philosophy sections
- Editorial aesthetic using Cormorant Garamond serif and modern sans typography with the neutral bone, ink, and warm terracotta palette from the prototype. Frontend alone for now.
